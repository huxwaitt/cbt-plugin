**Bottom line:** These measures answer narrower questions: is attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) likely, how severe is insomnia, how strong is eating disorder thinking, is drinking or drug use risky, and how impaired is daily life. Screeners such as the Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale (ASRS) and Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test (AUDIT) flag who needs fuller assessment. The Insomnia Severity Index (ISI) and Work and Social Adjustment Scale (WSAS) track change during cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT).

## What it measures

- **ASRS:** 18 adult ADHD symptom questions, with a 6-question screener [Kessler-etal-2005a]. The ASRS-5 is a 6-question screener updated for the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, fifth edition (DSM-5) [Ustun-etal-2017].
- **Conners Adult ADHD Rating Scales, 2nd Edition (CAARS 2):** adult self and observer forms, 97 items each, plus a 12-item ADHD Index [CAARS-2-Site]. **Conners 4th Edition (Conners 4):** parent (117 items), teacher (109) and self-report (118) forms for ages 6 to 18 [Conners-4-Site].
- **ISI:** insomnia severity, satisfaction, interference and distress over the last month [Morin-etal-2011].
- **Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI):** sleep quality over one month, as 7 component scores from 19 items [Buysse-etal-1989].
- **Eating Disorder Examination Questionnaire (EDE-Q):** self-report version of the Eating Disorder Examination interview [Fairburn-Beglin-1994], with four subscales and a global score [Mond-etal-2006].
- **AUDIT:** alcohol consumption, drinking behavior and alcohol-related problems in 10 items [Saunders-etal-1993]. AUDIT-C is its three consumption questions [Bradley-etal-2007].
- **Drug Abuse Screening Test (DAST):** consequences of drug use; 28 items originally [Skinner-1982], with 10- and 20-item versions [Yudko-etal-2007].
- **WSAS:** impairment in work, home management, social leisure, private leisure and close relationships [Mundt-etal-2002].
- **Clinical Outcomes in Routine Evaluation-10 (CORE-10):** general psychological distress, with six problem items, three functioning items and one risk item [CORE-Site].
- **Difficulties in Emotion Regulation Scale (DERS):** 36 items; brief form DERS-16 [Bjureberg-etal-2016].

## Format and scoring

| Measure | Format | Range |
|---|---|---|
| ISI | 7 items, 0 to 4 | 0 to 28 [Morin-etal-2011] |
| PSQI | 7 components summed to a global score | Global [Buysse-etal-1989] |
| AUDIT | 10 items, 0 to 4 (items 9 and 10 score 0, 2 or 4) | 0 to 40 [AUDIT-Site] |
| WSAS | 5 items, 0 (no impairment) to 8 (very severe) | 0 to 40 [Mundt-etal-2002] |
| CORE-10 | 10 items, 0 to 4; if one item is missing, mean of nine times 10; do not score if more are missing | 0 to 40 [CORE-Site] |

## Interpreting scores

- **ASRS screener:** general population sensitivity 68.7%, specificity 99.5% [Kessler-etal-2005a]. The ASRS-5 had sensitivity 91.4% and specificity 96.0% in population data, but specificity only 74.0% in a clinic sample [Ustun-etal-2017].
- **ISI:** 0 to 7 no insomnia, 8 to 14 subthreshold, 15 to 21 moderate, 22 to 28 severe. Cutoff 10 in community samples and 11 in clinical samples. A reduction of more than 7 points matched moderate improvement rated by an independent assessor [Morin-etal-2011].
- **PSQI:** a global score above 5 separated good and poor sleepers (sensitivity 89.6%, specificity 86.5%) [Buysse-etal-1989].
- **EDE-Q:** the global mean in young adult women was 1.52 (standard deviation 1.25) [Mond-etal-2006]. In community screening, a global score of 2.3 plus any objective binge episodes or exercise for weight control gave the best validity, from only 13 cases [Mond-etal-2004]. Factor analysis did not support the four subscales, so rely on the global score [Aardoom-etal-2012].
- **AUDIT:** 92% of hazardous or harmful drinkers scored 8 or more, and 94% of others scored under 8 [Saunders-etal-1993]. World Health Organization (WHO) manual zones are 0 to 7, 8 to 15, 16 to 19 and 20 to 40, from education to specialist referral [Babor-etal-2001]. The AUDIT website bands 8 to 14 hazardous or harmful and 15 or more likely dependence [AUDIT-Site].
- **AUDIT-C:** 4 or more in men and 3 or more in women [Bradley-etal-2007].
- **WSAS:** above 20 suggests moderately severe or worse problems, 10 to 20 significant impairment with less severe symptoms; the authors call these tentative [Mundt-etal-2002].
- **CORE-10:** clinical cutoff 11, reliable change 6 points [Barkham-etal-2013].

## Licensing and access

- CORE-10: free to reproduce unchanged with the copyright line kept, under a noncommercial no-derivatives license; independent translations are not allowed [CORE-Site].
- EDE-Q: copyrighted; free for noncommercial research use without permission; ask the developers about other uses [CREDO-Site].
- CAARS 2 and Conners 4: proprietary; buy from Multi-Health Systems (MHS) and do not reproduce items [CAARS-2-Site; Conners-4-Site].
- ASRS, ISI, PSQI, AUDIT, DAST and DERS: confirm current terms with the rights holder before electronic use.

## Guidance

- If an adult screens positive on the ASRS → say "This suggests it's worth a full ADHD assessment; it isn't a diagnosis," and refer or assess (see `adhd.assessment`). Because specificity fell to 74.0% in a clinic sample, so other conditions also screen positive [Ustun-etal-2017].
- If insomnia is a target → pair the ISI at baseline and end with a nightly sleep diary. Because a fall of more than 7 points marks moderate improvement [Morin-etal-2011], and the diary shows what changed.
- If the EDE-Q is elevated → assess weight, eating behaviors and medical risk in person (see `eating-disorders.medical-risk`). Because self-report runs higher than interview for binge eating and shape concern [Fairburn-Beglin-1994].
- If AUDIT-C is at or above the sex-specific cutoff → give the full AUDIT and discuss drinking. Because AUDIT-C is a screen for misuse, not a severity measure [Bradley-etal-2007].
- If symptom scores improve but WSAS stays above 20 → put work, relationships or leisure goals on the agenda. Because impairment is a separate target from symptoms [Mundt-etal-2002].
- If the CORE-10 risk item is endorsed → respond the same session (see `08-measurement.pitfall-unreviewed-risk-item`). Because it deliberately includes a risk item [CORE-Site].
