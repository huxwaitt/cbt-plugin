**Bottom line:** Process measures track how therapy is going, not only how the client is doing. The alliance predicts outcome modestly but robustly (r = 0.28 across 295 studies) [Fluckiger-etal-2018], so a brief end-of-session rating can catch ruptures early. Rate homework for both quantity and quality, because both relate to outcome in cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) [Kazantzis-etal-2016]. The Outcome Rating Scale (ORS) and Session Rating Scale (SRS) are free on paper for individual clinicians but may not be used electronically without a separate license [ORS-SRS-Site].

## What it measures

- **ORS:** four life domains: individual well-being, interpersonal relationships, social functioning and overall well-being; designed to track areas that change with therapy [ORS-SRS-Site].
- **SRS:** the therapeutic alliance in four areas: relationship quality, goals and topics, approach or method, and overall session quality [ORS-SRS-Site].
- **Working Alliance Inventory-Short Revised (WAI-SR):** agreement on goals, agreement on tasks, and the affective bond [Munder-etal-2010].
- **Homework ratings:** how much of the agreed between-session practice was done (quantity) and how well the skill was done (quality) [Kazantzis-etal-2016].

## Format and scoring

| Measure | Format | Scoring |
|---|---|---|
| ORS | 4 items, completed and scored in under a minute [ORS-SRS-Site] | 0 (worst) to 40 (best) [Kendrick-etal-2016] |
| SRS | 4 items at the end of the session [ORS-SRS-Site] | Higher means better fit |
| WAI-SR | Short self-report, bond, task and goal subscales | Reliability above 0.80 in outpatients and inpatients [Munder-etal-2010] |
| Homework | Clinician and client rating each session | Record percent of agreed tasks done and a quality rating |

The ORS and SRS form the Partners for Change Outcome Management System (PCOMS) [PCOMS-Site]. A child version of the ORS exists [PCOMS-Site].

## Interpreting scores

- **Alliance:** the alliance-outcome correlation is r = 0.278 face to face and 0.275 online, consistent across measures and rater perspectives [Fluckiger-etal-2018]. A single low score is a prompt to ask, not a verdict.
- **Change in alliance scores:** a drop from a client's usual level matters more than the absolute number, since many clients rate the alliance high.
- **ORS and SRS feedback:** feedback systems including PCOMS reduced deterioration and nearly doubled reliable or clinically significant change among clients predicted to do poorly [Lambert-etal-2018]. In CBT outpatients, ORS and SRS feedback led to similar outcomes in about two fewer sessions, with better outcome only for mood disorders [Janse-etal-2017].
- **Homework:** quantity of homework related to outcome at posttreatment (Hedges' g 0.79) and follow-up (g 0.51); quality related too (g 0.78 posttreatment, 1.07 follow-up), with wider confidence intervals from fewer studies [Kazantzis-etal-2016].

## Licensing and access

- ORS and SRS: a free individual license covers paper and pencil use only; "no electronic or digital use of the scales is permitted" under that license; groups pay tiered fees by number of providers; electronic use goes through authorized vendors [ORS-SRS-Site].
- WAI-SR: confirm terms with the developers before electronic use.
- Homework ratings you design yourself carry no license, but describe them in the record.

## Guidance

- If the SRS or alliance score drops below the client's usual level → raise it before they leave: "It looks like today's session fit a bit less well. What could I have done differently?" Then follow `alliance-and-stance.rupture-and-repair`. Because a stated invitation to criticize makes honest feedback safer, and the alliance predicts outcome [Fluckiger-etal-2018].
- If every SRS is at the ceiling → ask for one thing to change anyway: "Even if it was fine, what's one thing that would make next week more useful?" Because ceiling scores hide small problems.
- If homework was not done → rate it as zero without judgment, then explore barriers (see `05-session-structure.homework-review-and-adherence`). Because accurate quantity ratings predict outcome [Kazantzis-etal-2016] and hiding non-completion loses that signal.
- If homework was done → also rate how well the skill was used, for example whether a thought record included an alternative thought. Because quality of homework relates to outcome, not only quantity [Kazantzis-etal-2016].
- If you are building an app → do not put the ORS or SRS on screen under a free individual license. Because that license forbids electronic use [ORS-SRS-Site].
- If symptom scores are flat and the alliance is low → discuss the alliance first, covering bond, goals and tasks. Because these are the three parts the WAI-SR rates [Munder-etal-2010], and the alliance predicts outcome across treatments [Fluckiger-etal-2018].
