# 08-measurement: Measurement

## This stage
This domain covers how to measure in cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT): standardized symptom, functioning and process measures, personalized ratings, cutoffs, change statistics, licensing and measurement-based care. Measurement starts at assessment with a baseline, runs through every treatment session as routine outcome monitoring, and closes the episode with a classified outcome for the discharge summary.

## Decisions in order
1. Will you measure every session, and how will scores be reviewed and acted on in the session? → `08-measurement.measurement-based-care`
2. Which measures fit this presentation, age and setting? → `08-measurement.instrument-selection`
3. Does the license allow your intended paper, electronic or software use? → `08-measurement.instrument-licensing`
4. How do you score and interpret the depression measure? → `08-measurement.depression-measures`
5. How do you score and interpret the anxiety measure? → `08-measurement.anxiety-measures`
6. How do you score and interpret trauma or obsessive-compulsive measures? → `08-measurement.trauma-and-ocd-measures`
7. Do you need an attention, sleep, eating, substance, functioning or distress measure? → `08-measurement.other-measures`
8. What personal rating will track the client's own belief, distress or goal? → `08-measurement.idiographic-measures`
9. How will you track the alliance, session fit and homework? → `08-measurement.process-measures`
10. Is a score change real, and what is the end-of-treatment outcome? → `08-measurement.reliable-and-clinically-significant-change`

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Treating a Screening Score as a Diagnosis | A client is recorded as having depression, PTSD, ADHD or another disorder because a questionnaire passed its cutoff, with no clinical interview confirming it. | `08-measurement.pitfall-screening-score-as-diagnosis` |
| Missing an Endorsed Risk Item on a Questionnaire | A client marks thoughts of death or self-harm on the PHQ-9 or another measure, and nobody notices or asks about it until after the session or not at all. | `08-measurement.pitfall-unreviewed-risk-item` |
| Measuring Without Discussing or Acting | Questionnaires are collected every session but scores are filed unseen, never shown to the client, and flat or worsening trends run for weeks without any change to the plan. | `08-measurement.pitfall-measuring-without-acting` |
| Overreading Small Score Changes | A two- or three-point fall is celebrated as improvement, a similar rise triggers alarm, or success is declared from a percentage drop off a low baseline. | `08-measurement.pitfall-overreading-score-changes` |
