# 01-intake: Intake and Information Capture

## This stage
Everything a cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) therapist does and records from first contact until a treatment plan is agreed: consent, the risk screen, the presenting problem, history, structured diagnostic interviews, collateral information, goals and expectations, and socializing the client to CBT. It is the first stage of the episode of care and feeds formulation (`03-formulation`) and treatment planning (`04-treatment-planning`).

## Decisions in order
1. How do I run the first contact, and what must happen before depth (consent, confidentiality)? → `01-intake.first-session-structure`
2. Is anyone in danger right now, and does the risk screen require full assessment or escalation? → `01-intake.risk-screen-at-intake`
3. What exactly is the problem, and what does one recent episode look like? → `01-intake.presenting-problem`
4. What background (psychiatric, medical, substance, family, trauma, strengths) changes diagnosis, risk or treatment choice? → `01-intake.history-taking`
5. Do I need a structured diagnostic interview, and which one? → `01-intake.structured-interviews`
6. Do I need information from parents, partners or other providers, and how do I get valid consent? → `01-intake.collateral-information`
7. What does the client want from therapy, what do they expect, and how ready are they? → `01-intake.goals-and-expectations`
8. Does the client understand and agree to how CBT works? → `01-intake.socializing-to-cbt`
9. Is the intake record complete enough for someone else to pick up the case safely? → `01-intake.intake-record-checklist`

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| The Intake Becomes an Interrogation | The first session becomes a question-by-question form, no specific example or shared understanding emerges, and the client leaves without hope, a plan or reason to return. | `01-intake.pitfall-interrogation-intake` |
| Risk Screen Skipped, Vague or Not Acted On | Suicide and harm questions are left to a form or asked vaguely or late, and a positive answer is noted but not followed by assessment, planning or escalation. | `01-intake.pitfall-risk-screen-skipped` |
| Assessment Stays General, Never Specific | The client describes the problem only in sweeping terms, the therapist summarizes with labels, and intake ends with no concrete episode, thoughts or behaviors to formulate. | `01-intake.pitfall-general-not-specific` |
| Missed Medical, Medication, Substance or Bipolar Factors | CBT starts for anxiety, depression or insomnia while an untreated medical condition, medication effect, substance use or past manic episode keeps driving the symptoms. | `01-intake.pitfall-missed-medical-and-substance-factors` |
