# 11-conditions/panic-and-phobias: Panic Disorder, Agoraphobia and Specific Phobia

## This surface
Condition knowledge for panic disorder, agoraphobia and specific phobia: diagnosis, assessment, the cognitive and learning models that explain why fear persists, first-line cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) with interoceptive and in vivo exposure, one-session treatment for phobias, and adaptations. It covers assessment through ending for these conditions and draws on the exposure, safety behavior and measurement pages elsewhere. Any sign of suicide risk routes to `02-risk` first.

## Pages in order
1. `panic-and-phobias.overview`: start here: diagnostic essentials, prevalence, differentials and risk for all three conditions
2. `panic-and-phobias.assessment`: what to ask, screen and measure before formulating
3. `panic-and-phobias.cbt-model`: why panic and phobic fear persist, and how to draw it with the client
4. `panic-and-phobias.treatment`: the first-line CBT protocol for panic disorder, session by session
5. `panic-and-phobias.interoceptive-exposure`: the key panic component: bringing on feared body sensations to test predictions
6. `panic-and-phobias.agoraphobia`: diagnosis and exposure planning when several kinds of situations are avoided
7. `panic-and-phobias.specific-phobia`: subtypes, exposure, applied tension and medication for single-focus fears
8. `panic-and-phobias.one-session-treatment`: the brief intensive exposure protocol for specific phobia in adults and children
9. `panic-and-phobias.adaptations`: changes for comorbidity, severity, age, culture and medication, and when to step up or refer

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Exposure Stopped Before the Prediction Is Tested | Exposure tasks are cut short at peak fear, kept too easy, or ended once anxiety dips, so the feared outcome is never tested and avoidance returns. | `panic-and-phobias.pitfall-exposure-stopped-before-the-test` |
| Exposure With Hidden Safety Behaviors | The client completes exposure and anxiety drops, but catastrophic beliefs barely move and fear returns, because a companion, pill, breathing routine or other safety behavior stayed in use. | `panic-and-phobias.pitfall-hidden-safety-behaviors` |
| Reassurance Instead of Testing | The client keeps asking whether sensations are dangerous or seeks repeated tests, the therapist keeps reassuring, and the fear returns each time while panic does not improve. | `panic-and-phobias.pitfall-reassurance-instead-of-testing` |
