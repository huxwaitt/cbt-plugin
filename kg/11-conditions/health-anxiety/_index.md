# 11-conditions/health-anxiety: Health Anxiety

## This surface
Health anxiety in cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) terms: illness anxiety disorder and somatic symptom disorder in the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition, Text Revision (DSM-5-TR), and hypochondriasis and bodily distress disorder in the International Classification of Diseases, 11th Revision (ICD-11). These pages cover diagnosis, assessment and measures, the Salkovskis and Warwick maintenance model, the best-supported CBT protocol, responding to reassurance seeking, adaptations, and CBT for persistent physical symptoms. They apply from intake through ending; any sign of suicide risk routes to `02-risk` pages first, and new or changing physical symptoms route to medical review.

## Pages in order
1. `health-anxiety.overview`: start here for the diagnostic picture of illness anxiety disorder, somatic symptom disorder and ICD-11 hypochondriasis.
2. `health-anxiety.assessment`: what to ask, how to confirm medical status, and which measures and cutoffs to use.
3. `health-anxiety.cbt-model`: the maintenance model to draw with the client, built on the two competing explanations.
4. `health-anxiety.treatment`: the best-supported CBT protocol, session outline, formats and evidence.
5. `health-anxiety.reassurance-seeking`: how to respond in the moment when the client, family or a tool is asked "is this serious?".
6. `health-anxiety.adaptations`: decision rules for medical comorbidity, depression, poor insight, age, culture, medication and stepping up.
7. `health-anxiety.persistent-physical-symptoms`: when pain, fatigue or other persistent symptoms dominate rather than fear of a specific disease.

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Giving Reassurance That Feeds the Worry | The therapist, family or an app answers each "is this serious?" question, relief lasts hours or days, and requests return more often, about new symptoms, to more people. | `health-anxiety.pitfall-reassurance-loop` |
| Arguing That the Client Is Not Ill | Sessions become debates about medical facts, the therapist insists it is "just anxiety", the client feels dismissed and either drops out or seeks more tests elsewhere. | `health-anxiety.pitfall-debating-the-diagnosis` |
| Explaining Away New Physical Symptoms | Once a client is labeled health anxious, a new or changing symptom is put down to anxiety without medical review, or a care-avoidant client's overdue checks go unnoticed. | `health-anxiety.pitfall-missed-medical-illness` |
