**Bottom line:** Health anxiety does not protect anyone from real disease. A diagnosis of illness anxiety or somatic symptom disorder can sit alongside medical illness [Lowe-etal-2022], and people with hypochondriasis die of natural causes at a higher rate than matched controls [MataixCols-etal-2024]. Agree a medical review rule at the start of treatment so new symptoms are checked without restarting the reassurance cycle.

## What it looks like

- A client mentions weight loss or a new kind of pain, and the therapist treats it as another worry to sit with.
- Treatment notes say "health anxious" and other clinicians stop examining the client.
- A care-avoidant client has not had recommended screening for years and no one asks.
- The therapist feels unsure, so either ignores the symptom or encourages repeated tests; both lack a rule.

## Why it happens

The label shapes attention: once a formulation explains symptoms, new information is fitted to it. Therapists also fear that any medical referral will undo exposure work. Yet the cognitive behavioral approach itself requires that adequate investigation has been done and that, if symptoms do not fit the formulation, physical illness is reconsidered [Warwick-1998]. Care avoidance is common: in one clinic sample 14% avoided care and 61% swung between seeking and avoiding it [Newby-etal-2017].

## The fix

- If you are starting treatment → agree a written rule with the client and their doctor for new symptoms (for example, symptoms that are new, clearly different, worsening, or present with objective signs go to the doctor). Because a shared rule separates medical review from reassurance seeking (expert consensus).
- If symptoms do not fit the formulation → refer for medical review before continuing. Because the formulation should prompt reconsidering physical illness when it fails to fit [Warwick-1998].
- If the client avoids medical care → list overdue screening and build it into exposure. Because care-avoidant presentations are common [Newby-etal-2017].
- If the client has a known medical condition → coordinate with the treating clinician on which monitoring is needed. Because good care keeps somatic and psychological care connected [Henningsen-2018].
- If emergency signs are described → direct to emergency care at once (US 911; UK 999, or NHS 111 for urgent advice). Because safety comes before treatment rules (expert consensus).

Example phrasing: "This sounds different from the symptoms we have been working on, so it fits our rule for seeing your doctor. Let's book that, and keep practicing the plan for the old worries."

## Sources note

The coexistence of somatic symptom disorder with medical disease is part of the concept introduced in the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition (DSM-5), summarized in a scoping review [Lowe-etal-2022]. Mortality data come from a Swedish national cohort [MataixCols-etal-2024]. The written review rule is expert consensus.
