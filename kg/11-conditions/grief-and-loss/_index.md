# 11-conditions/grief-and-loss: Grief and Loss

## This surface
Grief after the death of someone close and prolonged grief disorder (PGD) in adults, with notes for children, adolescents and older adults. It covers the DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 diagnoses, how to tell normal and culturally expected mourning from PGD, assessment and measures (PG-13-R and the Inventory of Complicated Grief), the Boelen cognitive behavioral model, grief-focused cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) and prolonged grief disorder therapy, cultural variation in mourning, and suicide risk. Use it from assessment through ending; suicide procedures live in `02-risk`, trauma after violent deaths in `11-conditions/ptsd`, and general exposure and restructuring methods in `07-interventions`.

## Pages in order
1. `grief-and-loss.overview`: start here for DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 essentials, normal versus prolonged grief, prevalence, differentials and risk
2. `grief-and-loss.assessment`: take the loss history, check timing and cultural norms, rule out differentials, ask about suicide and set a PG-13-R or ICG baseline
3. `grief-and-loss.cbt-model`: build the shared Boelen formulation that explains why grief has stayed stuck
4. `grief-and-loss.treatment`: choose and deliver grief-focused CBT or prolonged grief disorder therapy and check the evidence
5. `grief-and-loss.adaptations`: adapt for culture and faith, age, violent or suicide loss, comorbidity, risk and remote delivery

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Treating Normal Grief as a Disorder | A recently bereaved person, or one grieving within their cultural norms, is diagnosed, booked for grief therapy or pushed to process the loss, though most people adapt without professional help. | `grief-and-loss.pitfall-treating-normal-grief` |
| Missing Suicide Risk Behind Longing for the Deceased | A client says they want to be with the person who died, and it is heard as ordinary longing, so suicidal thinking is never asked about or planned for. | `grief-and-loss.pitfall-missing-suicide-risk-in-grief` |
| Supportive Listening Without Facing the Loss | Sessions stay with comforting talk about the person, the story of the death and avoided reminders are never approached, and grief scores stay flat after months of therapy. | `grief-and-loss.pitfall-colluding-with-avoidance` |
