# 11-conditions/bipolar-and-psychosis: Bipolar Disorder and Psychosis

## This surface
Bipolar disorders, schizophrenia-spectrum psychosis and clinical high risk of psychosis, from recognition and assessment through formulation, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) as an add-on to medication, family work and relapse prevention. These pages span intake, assessment, formulation, planning and treatment, always inside a team that includes a prescriber. Start with the overview and assessment, choose the protocol on the treatment page, and check the pitfalls before using activation, sleep or belief-change techniques.

## Pages in order
1. `bipolar-and-psychosis.overview`: the diagnostic picture, differentials and suicide facts come first.
2. `bipolar-and-psychosis.assessment`: the mood timeline, experience mapping, screeners, collateral and same-day risk steps.
3. `bipolar-and-psychosis.at-risk-mental-state`: when experiences fall short of psychosis, preventive CBT replaces full CBT for psychosis.
4. `bipolar-and-psychosis.cbt-model`: the appraisal models turn the assessment into a shared formulation.
5. `bipolar-and-psychosis.treatment`: guideline status, dose and the phase outline for both conditions.
6. `bipolar-and-psychosis.cbtp`: the session-by-session CBT for psychosis protocol.
7. `bipolar-and-psychosis.bipolar-cbt`: relapse-prevention CBT and the relapse signature for bipolar disorder.
8. `bipolar-and-psychosis.family-work`: bringing relatives in to cut relapse in both conditions.
9. `bipolar-and-psychosis.adaptations`: changes for comorbidity, phase, age, culture, pregnancy, medication and non-response.

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Treating Bipolar Depression as Unipolar Depression | A client treated for "depression" improves briefly, then sleeps little, talks fast and spends, or cycles repeatedly, because past hypomania was never asked about and bipolar disorder was missed. | `bipolar-and-psychosis.pitfall-missed-bipolarity` |
| Arguing With or Colluding With Delusional Beliefs | The therapist tries to prove a persecutory belief false, or goes along with it to keep the peace; the client grows guarded, conviction holds, and sessions stall or end. | `bipolar-and-psychosis.pitfall-arguing-with-delusions` |
| Unmonitored Sleep Restriction and Activation in Bipolar Disorder | Standard sleep restriction, late-night homework or vigorous activity scheduling is used with a bipolar client, sleep falls and energy climbs, and the client tips into hypomania without anyone noticing. | `bipolar-and-psychosis.pitfall-sleep-loss-and-activation` |
