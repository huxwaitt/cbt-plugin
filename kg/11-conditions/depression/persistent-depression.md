**Bottom line:** Persistent depressive disorder (PDD) means depressed mood on most days for 2 years or more [Coryell-2026]. Chronic depression responds less to short treatment, so plan longer therapy (about 18 or more sessions) and discuss combining psychotherapy with medication [Cuijpers-etal-2010; Keller-etal-2000]. The Cognitive Behavioral Analysis System of Psychotherapy (CBASP) was built for chronic depression and has modest advantages over supportive therapy [Schramm-etal-2017; Negt-etal-2016].

## When to use

- Depressed mood most days for at least 2 years (1 year in young people), with at least two associated symptoms and no symptom-free spell of 2 months or more [Coryell-2026].
- PDD includes dysthymia with or without major depressive episodes, chronic major depression, and recurrent depression without full recovery [Schramm-etal-2020].
- International Classification of Diseases, 11th Revision (ICD-11) dysthymic disorder excludes a depressive episode in its first 2 years [WHO-CDDR-2024].
- The United Kingdom (UK) National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) uses "chronic depressive symptoms" for 2 years or more of major depression, subthreshold symptoms or low mood [NICE-NG222].

## How it works

McCullough's CBASP treats chronic depression as an interpersonal problem. The client acts without seeing how their behavior shapes what happens to them, so outcomes feel out of their control [McCullough-2000; McCullough-2003]. Treatment teaches the client to link their interpretations and actions to real consequences. It uses three core methods: Situational Analysis, the Interpersonal Discrimination Exercise, and behavioral skill training and rehearsal [McCullough-2003]. The therapist also uses disciplined personal involvement (planned, honest personal responses) [McCullough-2000].

Chronic-focused cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) should cover avoidance, rumination and interpersonal difficulties [NICE-NG222].

## Steps

1. **Assess the course.** Draw a mood timeline since childhood: onset, worst and best periods, treatments. Ask "When did you last feel well for two months or more?"
2. **Socialize to a longer plan.** Agree on a course of about 18 or more sessions with continuation sessions [Cuijpers-etal-2010]. CBASP trials used 16 to 20 sessions over 12 weeks [Keller-etal-2000] or 24 sessions over 20 weeks plus 8 continuation sessions [Schramm-etal-2017].
3. **Significant Other History** (CBASP): list key people from the past and what the client learned from each about relationships [McCullough-2000].
4. **Situational Analysis, every session** [McCullough-2003]:
   - Describe one recent, specific interpersonal situation with a clear start and end.
   - List the client's interpretations of it.
   - Describe what they did and said.
   - State the actual outcome.
   - State the desired outcome, which must be realistic and within the client's control.
   - Compare the two outcomes. Then revise each interpretation and behavior: "Did this help or hinder you in getting what you wanted?"
5. **Interpersonal Discrimination Exercise:** when the client expects the therapist to react like a hurtful figure from their past, help them notice how the therapist actually responded [McCullough-2003].
6. **Skill training and rehearsal:** assertiveness and other skills that the analyses show are missing [McCullough-2003].
7. **Target rumination and avoidance** in chronic-focused CBT. Rumination-focused CBT helped medication-refractory residual depression [Watkins-etal-2011].
8. **Coordinate medication** and plan boosters.

## Guidance

- If the client says "This is just who I am" → validate how long it has lasted, then name it as depression that can be treated (see `responding-in-session.responding-to-hopelessness-and-self-criticism`). Because people with chronic depressive symptoms may never have sought treatment or recognized it as depression [NICE-NG222].
- If the desired outcome depends on others changing → help the client restate it as something they can do. Because CBASP needs outcomes within the client's control [McCullough-2003].
- If the client has severe chronic depression and has not tried medication → discuss combination treatment with a prescriber. Because combination beat either treatment alone in a large trial [Keller-etal-2000].
- If CBASP is added after medication nonresponse → set realistic expectations and review often. Because adding CBASP or supportive therapy did not beat optimized medication alone [Kocsis-etal-2009].
- If progress is slow → review the formulation, comorbidity and alliance before ending. Because chronic depression often needs longer treatment [Cuijpers-etal-2010].

Example phrasings:
- "You've carried this a long time, so it feels like part of you. It is also something we can work on."
- "In that conversation, what did you want to happen? Was that something you could make happen?"
- Avoid: "You're not trying hard enough." It repeats the hostile experiences that CBASP aims to correct.

## Common problems

- **Vague situations:** keep to one slice of time with a clear end point.
- **Unrealistic desired outcomes:** "I wanted him to love me" becomes "I wanted to tell him I was hurt."
- **Therapist frustration:** passivity can pull you into rescuing or criticizing; use supervision.

## Evidence

- In 681 adults with chronic depression, response was 48% with nefazodone or CBASP alone and 73% with the combination [Keller-etal-2000].
- CBASP beat supportive psychotherapy at 20 weeks (Cohen's d = 0.31) and 48 weeks (d = 0.39) in unmedicated early-onset chronic depression [Schramm-etal-2017].
- Meta-analysis of 6 trials (1,510 patients): small overall advantage for CBASP (g = 0.34 to 0.44), similar to medication, with combination better than medication alone [Negt-etal-2016].
- Across 16 trials, psychotherapy had a small effect (d = 0.23), was less effective than medication for dysthymia, and needed about 18 sessions for optimal effects [Cuijpers-etal-2010].
- Guidelines: NICE recommends CBT focused on chronic symptoms, or medication, or both, and does not name CBASP [NICE-NG222]. The American Psychological Association (APA) found insufficient evidence for or against CBASP and did not recommend adding it for antidepressant partial responders, citing burden and limited added benefit [APA-Depression-Guideline-2019]. The United States (US) Departments of Veterans Affairs and Defense (VA/DoD) guideline suggests combination treatment for depression lasting more than 2 years [VA-DoD-MDD-2022]. Evidence strength: supported by randomized trials, with modest effects.
