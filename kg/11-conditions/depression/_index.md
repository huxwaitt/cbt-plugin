# 11-conditions/depression: Depression

## This surface
Major depressive disorder and persistent depressive disorder in adults, with adaptations for young people, older adults, pregnancy and culture. It covers diagnosis, assessment and measures, the cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) maintenance model, the first-line CBT protocol, chronic depression and the Cognitive Behavioral Analysis System of Psychotherapy (CBASP), and relapse prevention including mindfulness-based cognitive therapy (MBCT). Use it from assessment through ending; risk procedures live in `02-risk` and general methods in `03-formulation` and `07-interventions`.

## Pages in order
1. `depression.overview`: start here for the diagnostic picture, course, differentials and risk notes.
2. `depression.assessment`: confirm diagnosis, severity, risk and bipolarity, and set a measurement baseline.
3. `depression.cbt-model`: build the shared maintenance formulation that sets treatment targets.
4. `depression.treatment`: deliver the first-line CBT protocol and check it against guidelines.
5. `depression.adaptations`: change delivery for severity, comorbidity, age, pregnancy, culture or medication, and decide when to step up.
6. `depression.persistent-depression`: use when symptoms have lasted 2 years or more or standard treatment has not worked.
7. `depression.relapse-prevention-mbct`: after remission, prevent the next episode with MBCT or relapse-focused CBT.

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Thought Challenging Before Activation | A slowed, hopeless client is given thought records in the first sessions, cannot complete them, and reads the failure as more proof of being useless. | `depression.pitfall-thought-challenging-too-early` |
| Suicide Risk Checked Once and Not Again | Suicide risk is screened at intake and then not reassessed as hopelessness, agitation, medication changes or new losses shift it during depression treatment. | `depression.pitfall-risk-not-rechecked` |
| Ending at Response With Residual Symptoms | Therapy ends once the client feels somewhat better, with residual symptoms, no remission check and no written relapse plan, and depression returns within months. | `depression.pitfall-stopping-at-response` |
