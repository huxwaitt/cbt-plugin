**Bottom line:** Suicide risk in depression moves. It can rise early in treatment, when medication starts or changes, when agitation appears, and after new losses [NICE-NG222]. Check suicidal thinking at every session (for example with Patient Health Questionnaire-9 (PHQ-9) item 9 plus a direct question), act on any change the same day, and record what you asked and did.

## What it looks like

- A thorough risk assessment at intake, then no further questions for weeks because "risk was low."
- PHQ-9 item 9 rises from 0 to 1 and is noted in the file but not discussed.
- The client says "nothing is ever going to change" and the therapist moves straight to disputing the thought.
- The client started an antidepressant last week and reports feeling restless; nobody asks about suicidal thoughts.
- A breakup or job loss is mentioned at the end of the session and not followed up.

## Why it happens

- Risk is treated as an intake task rather than a running measurement.
- Therapists fear that asking repeatedly will upset the client or plant the idea.
- Questionnaires are filed without being read in session.
- Hopelessness sounds like an ordinary depressive thought, so its risk meaning is missed. Yet hopelessness predicted eventual suicide over 5 to 10 years of follow-up in hospitalized patients [Beck-etal-1985].

## The fix

- If PHQ-9 item 9 is above 0, or hopelessness is voiced → ask directly about suicidal thoughts, intent and plans in that session before other work. Because the United Kingdom (UK) National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) says to always ask directly about suicidal ideation and intent [NICE-NG222].
- If the client has just started or changed an antidepressant, or reports agitation → ask about suicidal thoughts and make sure they know how to get help quickly. Because agitation, anxiety and suicidal ideation can rise in the early stages of treatment [NICE-NG222].
- If risk has risen → increase contact (in person, video or phone), review or build a safety plan that includes restricting access to lethal means, involve supports with consent, and consider specialist referral. Because NICE lists these steps and says not to withhold depression treatment because of risk [NICE-NG222].
- If there is imminent danger → do not leave the client alone; contact emergency services. United States (US): 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988) or 911. UK: 999, NHS 111 (National Health Service) or Samaritans 116 123.
- If a new loss or crisis is mentioned late in the session → extend or schedule a same-day check. Because personal stress is a named high-risk period [NICE-NG222].

Example phrasing: "Last week you said things felt hopeless. Have you had any thoughts of ending your life since we last met?"

Avoid: "You're not thinking of doing anything silly, are you?" It minimizes and invites a "no."

Record at each session: PHQ-9 total and item 9, the direct question asked and the answer, changes since last session, actions taken and who was informed.

## Sources note

Monitoring rules come from NICE [NICE-NG222]. The VA/DoD (United States Departments of Veterans Affairs and Defense) guideline counts suicidal ideation on the PHQ-9 at "several days" or more and advises at least monthly monitoring after treatment starts [VA-DoD-MDD-2022]. The PHQ-9 is a validated severity measure [Kroenke-etal-2001]; item 9 prompts a risk assessment but does not replace one. Evidence strength: guideline-recommended and expert consensus. See `02-risk.ongoing-risk-monitoring` for structured tools.
