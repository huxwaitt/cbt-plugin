# 11-conditions/ptsd: PTSD and Trauma

## This surface
Posttraumatic stress disorder (PTSD), acute stress disorder and ICD-11 complex PTSD in adults, children and adolescents: diagnosis, assessment and measurement, the cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) maintenance models, and the first-line trauma-focused treatments (cognitive processing therapy, prolonged exposure and cognitive therapy for PTSD) with their guideline status. It spans assessment through ending for trauma presentations and links to `08-measurement` for instruments, `02-risk` for suicide and safeguarding, and `07-interventions` for general exposure and restructuring skills.

## Pages in order
1. `ptsd.overview`: start here for DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 essentials, prevalence, course, differentials and risk
2. `ptsd.assessment`: screen, take the trauma history, confirm the diagnosis and set up session-by-session PCL-5 monitoring
3. `ptsd.early-intervention`: use instead of the treatment pages when the trauma was within about the last month
4. `ptsd.cbt-model`: build the shared Ehlers and Clark formulation that explains why symptoms persist
5. `ptsd.treatment`: choose a first-line trauma-focused protocol, plan the course and check guideline status
6. `ptsd.cognitive-processing-therapy`: deliver cognitive processing therapy session by session
7. `ptsd.prolonged-exposure`: deliver prolonged exposure session by session
8. `ptsd.cognitive-therapy-for-ptsd`: deliver Ehlers and Clark cognitive therapy for PTSD
9. `ptsd.complex-ptsd`: assess ICD-11 complex PTSD and decide how to sequence treatment
10. `ptsd.adaptations`: adapt for comorbidity, risk, dissociation, culture, medication and access, and decide when to step up or refer
11. `ptsd.children-and-adolescents`: treat PTSD in children and adolescents with caregivers

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Indefinite Stabilization Before Trauma Work | Months of coping skills, grounding and supportive sessions pass, the trauma memory is never processed because the client never seems stable enough, and PTSD symptoms stay unchanged. | `ptsd.pitfall-indefinite-stabilization` |
| Therapist Avoidance of the Trauma Memory | Sessions drift into general support or problem-solving, imaginal exposure is shortened, hot spots and stuck points are skipped, and the protocol is delivered in name only. | `ptsd.pitfall-therapist-avoidance-of-trauma-work` |
| Routine Debriefing or Early Treatment for Everyone Exposed | After an incident everyone is asked to talk through the event in one session, or offered therapy regardless of symptoms, while people with real early disorder go unfollowed. | `ptsd.pitfall-routine-debriefing` |
