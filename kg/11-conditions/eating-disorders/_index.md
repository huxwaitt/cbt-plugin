# 11-conditions/eating-disorders: Eating Disorders

## This surface
Condition-specific knowledge for anorexia nervosa, bulimia nervosa, binge-eating disorder, other specified eating disorders and, briefly, avoidant/restrictive food intake disorder (ARFID): diagnosis, assessment, medical risk, the transdiagnostic cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) model, enhanced CBT (CBT-E) and guideline-based adaptations. It is used from assessment through ending, alongside the generic pages in `01-intake`, `03-formulation` and `08-measurement`. Physical danger signs route to `eating-disorders.medical-risk` first, and suicide risk to `02-risk`.

## Pages in order
1. `eating-disorders.overview`: start here: what each eating disorder is, ICD-11 differences, course and mortality
2. `eating-disorders.assessment`: screen, take the eating and weight history, weigh, measure and gather collateral
3. `eating-disorders.medical-risk`: run alongside assessment and every session: danger signs and when to escalate
4. `eating-disorders.cbt-model`: build the transdiagnostic formulation that sets the targets
5. `eating-disorders.treatment`: choose the protocol by diagnosis and age, plan its length, know the evidence
6. `eating-disorders.cbt-e-stages`: deliver CBT-E stage by stage
7. `eating-disorders.adaptations`: adjust for age, severity, comorbidity, culture, diabetes, ARFID and medication, and step up

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Avoiding or Hiding In-Session Weighing | The therapist skips weighing, weighs "blind," or leaves weight to the client or another clinician, so weight fears, checking and unexplained weight loss go unaddressed in therapy. | `eating-disorders.pitfall-avoiding-in-session-weighing` |
| Assuming Normal Weight Means Low Risk | A client at average or higher weight who restricts, purges or lost weight fast is told they are not ill enough, goes unchecked medically, or waits behind thinner clients. | `eating-disorders.pitfall-normal-weight-assumed-safe` |
| Drifting from the Eating Disorder Protocol | Sessions slide into general support, waiting for motivation, or talk about history, while regular eating, monitoring, weighing and food experiments quietly stop and symptoms stay the same. | `eating-disorders.pitfall-therapist-drift` |
| Setting Weight-Loss Goals During Binge-Eating Treatment | A diet or calorie target is added to treatment for binge eating or bulimia nervosa, binges continue or rebound, and success is judged by the scale instead of eating behavior. | `eating-disorders.pitfall-weight-loss-goals-in-binge-eating` |
