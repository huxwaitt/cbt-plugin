# 11-conditions/social-anxiety: Social Anxiety Disorder

## This surface
Social anxiety disorder from recognition to the end of treatment: diagnosis, assessment and measures, the Clark and Wells formulation, cognitive therapy for social anxiety disorder and its signature techniques, and adaptations. Pages run from the assessment stage through formulation, treatment and ending. Generic methods live in the formulation, intervention and measurement domains; these pages apply them to social anxiety.

## Pages in order
1. `social-anxiety.overview`: what the disorder is and how it differs from look-alikes, before anything else.
2. `social-anxiety.assessment`: screen, confirm and measure severity so treatment can be tracked.
3. `social-anxiety.cbt-model`: draw the shared Clark and Wells formulation that every technique targets.
4. `social-anxiety.treatment`: deliver the first-line individual protocol session by session.
5. `social-anxiety.video-feedback-and-attention-training`: correct the distorted self-image and reverse self-focus early in treatment.
6. `social-anxiety.behavioral-experiments-and-surveys`: run the experiments that carry most of the change from session 4 onward.
7. `social-anxiety.adaptations`: adjust for comorbidity, age, culture, autism and medication, and decide when to step up.

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Exposure That Leaves Safety Behaviors in Place | The client keeps entering feared social situations, anxiety eases a little, but belief in the feared outcome stays high because rehearsing, hiding or self-monitoring continued throughout. | `social-anxiety.pitfall-safety-behaviors-during-exposure` |
| Showing Video Feedback Without Preparation | The therapist plays a recording without predictions or viewing instructions, the client watches through their feelings and inner critic, spots flaws, and leaves more convinced they look odd. | `social-anxiety.pitfall-unprepared-video-feedback` |
| Mistaking Safety Behaviors for a Skills Deficit | The therapist sees a quiet, stilted client, assumes poor social skills, and coaches scripts and conversation techniques that become new safety behaviors while fear of evaluation stays untouched. | `social-anxiety.pitfall-skills-deficit-assumption` |
| Defaulting to Brief, Group or Low-Intensity Formats | Social anxiety disorder is routed by default to a short course of standard sessions, a general anxiety group or self-help, and the client drops out or improves little. | `social-anxiety.pitfall-brief-or-group-default` |
