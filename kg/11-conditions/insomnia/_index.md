# 11-conditions/insomnia: Insomnia Disorder

## This surface
Insomnia disorder from recognition to the end of treatment: diagnosis under DSM-5-TR and ICD-11, assessment and measures, the sleep diary, the 3P and cognitive maintenance models, cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) and its components, brief and digital formats, and adaptations for comorbidity and special groups. Pages run from assessment through formulation, treatment and ending, and also cover insomnia as a comorbid problem in other conditions. Generic methods live in the formulation, intervention and measurement domains; these pages apply them to sleep.

## Pages in order
1. `insomnia.overview`: what insomnia disorder is, how DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 define it, and what it must be told apart from.
2. `insomnia.assessment`: confirm the diagnosis, screen for other sleep disorders and risk, and measure severity.
3. `insomnia.sleep-diary`: collect baseline sleep data and calculate the sleep efficiency every later decision depends on.
4. `insomnia.cbt-model`: draw the shared 3P and cognitive formulation that every technique targets.
5. `insomnia.treatment`: deliver the first-line CBT-I protocol session by session and know its guideline status.
6. `insomnia.cbt-i-components`: apply sleep restriction, stimulus control, cognitive therapy and arousal reduction with safe titration rules.
7. `insomnia.brief-and-digital-cbt-i`: choose a brief, digital or group format when full in-person CBT-I is not available or wanted.
8. `insomnia.adaptations`: adjust or delay for comorbidity, age, culture, sleepiness and medication, and decide when to step up or refer.

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Sleep Hygiene Advice Offered as the Treatment | A client with chronic insomnia gets a sleep hygiene leaflet or tips about caffeine and screens, follows them, still sleeps badly, and concludes that therapy cannot help. | `insomnia.pitfall-sleep-hygiene-only` |
| Sleep Restriction Without Safety Screening | A sleep window is prescribed without checking for sleep apnea, bipolar disorder, seizures, sleepiness or driving, and the client becomes dangerously sleepy, has a mood episode or drops out. | `insomnia.pitfall-unscreened-sleep-restriction` |
| Treating Comorbid Insomnia as Just a Symptom | Insomnia alongside depression, anxiety, PTSD or pain is noted as a symptom and left to resolve with the main disorder, and it persists after that disorder improves. | `insomnia.pitfall-insomnia-treated-as-a-symptom` |
| Judging Progress by Hours Slept | Client and therapist track only total hours of sleep, see little change during CBT-I, extend time in bed to chase more sleep, and abandon a treatment that was working. | `insomnia.pitfall-judging-progress-by-hours` |
