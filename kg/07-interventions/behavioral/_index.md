# 07-interventions/behavioral: Behavioral and Exposure Interventions

## This surface
Techniques that change problems by changing behavior: activating clients with depression, solving practical problems, teaching skills, and exposure-based work that reduces avoidance and safety behaviors in anxiety, obsessive-compulsive and trauma-related problems. These pages sit in the treatment stage, after formulation and planning, and link out to the condition protocols that use them.

## Pages in order
1. `behavioral.behavioral-activation`: Start here for depression with withdrawal or avoidance; sets the rationale and functional analysis.
2. `behavioral.activity-monitoring-and-scheduling`: The diary and scheduling procedure that every activation plan runs on.
3. `behavioral.problem-solving-therapy`: When practical problems, not just inactivity, keep low mood or stress going.
4. `behavioral.skills-training`: When a missing skill (assertiveness, social, organization) blocks progress.
5. `behavioral.exposure-principles`: Read before any exposure: what each trial should teach and when to end it.
6. `behavioral.exposure-hierarchy`: Build the rated, graded list of exposure tasks.
7. `behavioral.exposure-types`: Choose in vivo, imaginal or interoceptive exposure to match the feared stimulus.
8. `behavioral.exposure-and-response-prevention`: Apply exposure to obsessions and block rituals and reassurance.
9. `behavioral.safety-behavior-reduction`: Find and drop the behaviors that stop exposure from disconfirming fears.
10. `behavioral.behavioral-experiments`: Test a specific belief or prediction through planned experience.
11. `behavioral.relaxation-and-arousal-reduction`: Decide when relaxation and breathing help and when they act as safety behaviors.

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Scheduling Pleasant Activities Without Functional Analysis | The client's diary fills with scheduled activities but mood stays flat, tasks feel like chores, key avoidance continues, and rumination runs through everything they do. | `behavioral.pitfall-activity-without-function` |
| Treating Fear Reduction as the Goal of Exposure | Exposure trials end only once anxiety drops, sessions with high fear are counted as failures, clients say they must feel calm first, and gains fade in new settings. | `behavioral.pitfall-chasing-habituation` |
| Delivering Exposure Too Cautiously | Exposure stalls at low hierarchy items, the therapist allows escape, distraction or reassurance, top items are never attempted, and sessions drift into talk about anxiety. | `behavioral.pitfall-overly-cautious-exposure` |
| Missing Subtle Safety Behaviors | The client completes exposures or experiments yet beliefs barely shift, because hidden checking, reassurance, distraction, relaxation or mental rituals got the credit for safety. | `behavioral.pitfall-unnoticed-safety-behaviors` |
