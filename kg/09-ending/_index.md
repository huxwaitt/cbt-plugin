# 09-ending: Ending and Relapse Prevention

## This stage
This domain covers the last phase of an episode of care: deciding when to end, preparing the client from the first session, consolidating gains in a written relapse prevention plan, offering boosters or continuation sessions, and closing the record. It also covers endings that do not go to plan, including dropout, referral on and handover to other providers. It follows treatment and links back to measurement, risk and ethics.

## Decisions in order
1. How will the ending be built into the plan from the first session, and how will the final phase run? → `09-ending.planning-the-ending`
2. Is it time to end, extend for relapse prevention, or end and refer? → `09-ending.ending-criteria`
3. What words will we use for setbacks, lapses and relapse? → `09-ending.lapse-relapse-and-recurrence`
4. What goes in the client's written plan for staying well and handling setbacks? → `09-ending.relapse-prevention-blueprint`
5. Should boosters or a continuation phase follow, and how are they run? → `09-ending.booster-sessions`
6. The client has stopped attending or wants to stop abruptly: what now? → `09-ending.dropout-and-unplanned-endings`
7. The client needs care elsewhere: how is it referred and handed over safely? → `09-ending.referral-and-handover`
8. How is the episode closed and documented? → `09-ending.discharge-summary`

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| The Abrupt Ending | The last weekly session arrives with no tapering, no written relapse plan and no route back; the client leaves anxious, credits the therapist, and returns in crisis months later. | `09-ending.pitfall-abrupt-ending` |
| Ending at Response, Not Remission | Scores have dropped and the client feels better, so therapy ends while poor sleep, avoidance, rumination or low energy remain; months later the full problem returns. | `09-ending.pitfall-ending-with-residual-symptoms` |
| Letting Dropout Go Silent | A client stops attending, one voicemail is left, and the file is closed weeks later with no risk check, no closing letter, no summary and no route back. | `09-ending.pitfall-silent-dropout` |
