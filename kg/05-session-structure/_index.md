# 05-session-structure: Session Structure and Documentation

## This stage
This domain covers how each cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) treatment session is run and recorded: the standard sequence, mood check and bridge, agenda, homework design and review, summaries and feedback, and session notes. It also covers therapist competence and adapting sessions for video or phone. It applies to every session in the treatment stage, after intake and formulation and before ending.

## Decisions in order
1. How will this session run, and when should I depart from the usual sequence? → `05-session-structure.session-structure`
2. How has the client been since last time, and does anything (including risk) change today's plan? → `05-session-structure.mood-check-and-bridge`
3. Which one or two problems will we work on today, in what order? → `05-session-structure.agenda-setting`
4. How did last week's action plan go, and what do I do if it was not done? → `05-session-structure.homework-review-and-adherence`
5. What between-session task follows from today's work, and how do I make it doable? → `05-session-structure.homework-design`
6. Do we share the same understanding, and how did the session land for the client? → `05-session-structure.session-summaries-and-feedback`
7. What goes in the note, and where? → `05-session-structure.session-notes`
8. Is the session remote, and what extra safety and privacy routines are needed? → `05-session-structure.telehealth-adaptations`
9. How skillfully am I delivering this, and how do I improve? → `05-session-structure.therapist-competence`

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Sessions That Drift Into Weekly Recaps | Sessions are spent recounting the week, jump between topics, end without an intervention or action plan, and the client feels heard but nothing changes. | `05-session-structure.pitfall-unstructured-drift` |
| Structure Imposed Rigidly | The therapist enforces agenda, forms and timing mechanically, interrupts too much, overrides the client's distress, and the client becomes irritated, silent or drops out. | `05-session-structure.pitfall-rigid-structure` |
| Homework Assigned but Not Reviewed | Tasks are set vaguely in the last minute, never asked about next session, and completion steadily falls until the client stops doing anything between sessions. | `05-session-structure.pitfall-homework-assigned-not-reviewed` |
| Session Notes That Say Nothing | Progress notes read "discussed stressors, provided CBT", carry no scores, plan or risk status, are copied from last week, or mix private process reflections into the shared record. | `05-session-structure.pitfall-uninformative-session-notes` |
