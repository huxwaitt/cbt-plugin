# 10-diagnosis: Diagnosis and Classification

## This stage
How a cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) therapist uses the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition, Text Revision (DSM-5-TR) and the International Classification of Diseases (ICD) as working tools: reading criteria, reasoning through a differential diagnosis, handling comorbidity, assessing culture, sharing a diagnosis, coding it, and staying within scope of practice. It sits in assessment, after the intake (`01-intake`) has gathered history and risk, and before formulation (`03-formulation`) and treatment planning (`04-treatment-planning`).

## Decisions in order
1. What does DSM-5-TR require, and how do criteria, specifiers and residual categories work? → `10-diagnosis.dsm-5-tr-overview`
2. Am I allowed to make this diagnosis in my license and jurisdiction? → `10-diagnosis.scope-of-practice-diagnosis`
3. What diagnosis, if any, best explains the symptoms once substance and medical causes are excluded? → `10-diagnosis.differential-diagnosis-process`
4. How do culture, language and context shape the presentation and its meaning? → `10-diagnosis.cultural-formulation-interview`
5. Does the client meet criteria for more than one disorder, and what do we treat first? → `10-diagnosis.comorbidity`
6. What does the diagnosis add to the formulation, and what must the formulation supply? → `10-diagnosis.diagnosis-vs-formulation`
7. How do I tell the client, and what will they and others see? → `10-diagnosis.communicating-a-diagnosis`
8. Which code goes on the record or claim, and how does ICD-11 differ? → `10-diagnosis.icd-10-cm-and-icd-11`

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Premature Diagnostic Closure | The first diagnosis that fits, often depression or anxiety, is never questioned, so bipolar disorder, substance or medical causes and second disorders go unseen while treatment stalls. | `10-diagnosis.pitfall-premature-diagnostic-closure` |
| Treating a Questionnaire Score as a Diagnosis | A PHQ-9, GAD-7 or other screening total above a cutoff is recorded or told to the client as a diagnosis, without a clinical interview of criteria, duration, impairment and exclusions. | `10-diagnosis.pitfall-questionnaire-score-as-diagnosis` |
| Culture Misread as Pathology | Culturally normal beliefs, idioms of distress or justified mistrust are read as symptoms, mood symptoms are underweighted, and racialized clients receive psychosis diagnoses more often than warranted. | `10-diagnosis.pitfall-culture-misread-as-pathology` |
| Diagnosis Shaped by Billing or Outside Scope | The recorded diagnosis is chosen to secure payment, more sessions or less stigma rather than from the assessment, or is assigned by someone whose license does not allow it. | `10-diagnosis.pitfall-diagnosis-shaped-by-billing` |
