# 02-risk: Risk and Safety

## This stage
This domain covers suicide and self-harm risk, risk of harm to others, safeguarding, crisis response and risk documentation. Risk work starts at intake and runs through every stage of care, including ending. Read it before any session with a client who has had suicidal thoughts, self-harm or violent ideation. Crisis contacts: US 988 (call or text) or 911; UK 999, National Health Service (NHS) 111 (mental health option) or Samaritans 116 123.

## Decisions in order
1. How do I raise suicide with this client, in words that invite an honest answer? → `02-risk.asking-about-suicide`
2. Which screen do I use, and what does a positive answer require? → `02-risk.c-ssrs`
3. What is driving this client's risk now, and what reduces it? → `02-risk.suicide-risk-assessment`
4. Is the risk imminent, and what do I do before the client leaves? → `02-risk.crisis-response`
5. What will the client do in the next crisis, and how do we make means safer? → `02-risk.safety-planning`
6. Is the client hurting themselves without suicidal intent, and what does it do for them? → `02-risk.self-harm`
7. Has the client threatened someone, and does a duty to warn or protect apply? → `02-risk.harm-to-others`
8. Has abuse of a child or an adult at risk been disclosed, and where must I report? → `02-risk.safeguarding`
9. How should treatment target suicidality itself? → `02-risk.suicide-focused-cbt`
10. What do I write down about the assessment and the decision? → `02-risk.risk-documentation`
11. How do I check risk each session and act on PHQ-9 item 9? → `02-risk.ongoing-risk-monitoring`

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Avoiding or Softening the Suicide Question | The clinician skips asking about suicide, asks vaguely or in a way that invites "no", or changes the subject quickly when the client hints at not wanting to live. | `02-risk.pitfall-avoiding-the-question` |
| Leaving a Positive Suicide Screen Unaddressed | A client marks PHQ-9 item 9 or a suicide screener item positive, and the score is filed, averaged into a total, or noticed only after the client has left. | `02-risk.pitfall-unaddressed-positive-screen` |
| Treating a Risk Label or Score as a Prediction | A client rated "low risk" by a checklist or score is discharged, not followed up, or not offered treatment, and the label replaces a formulation and a plan. | `02-risk.pitfall-risk-label-as-prediction` |
| Relying on No-Suicide Contracts | The clinician asks a suicidal client to promise, verbally or in writing, not to harm themselves, and treats the promise as a safety measure or as legal protection. | `02-risk.pitfall-no-suicide-contracts` |
