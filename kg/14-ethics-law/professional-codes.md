**Bottom line:** Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) is practiced by many professions, so the code that binds you depends on your license or membership, not on the therapy you deliver. In the United States (US) that is usually the American Psychological Association (APA), American Counseling Association (ACA) or National Association of Social Workers (NASW) code plus your state board's rules; in the United Kingdom (UK) it is the British Association for Behavioural and Cognitive Psychotherapies (BABCP), the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP), or the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC) for psychologists. Codes agree on most things but differ on details such as relationships with former clients.

## What it is

| Code | Who it binds | Current edition (checked 2026-11-06) |
|---|---|---|
| APA Ethical Principles and Code of Conduct | APA members; adopted by many psychology boards | 2002, amended 2010 and 2016, effective January 1, 2017; check apa.org for any newer edition [APA-Ethics-2017] |
| ACA Code of Ethics | Counselors | 2014 remains current; a revised code went out for public comment in spring 2026 and the final version was due in fall 2026 [ACA-Ethics-Site] |
| NASW Code of Ethics | Social workers | 2021 update [NASW-Code-2021] |
| BABCP Standards of Conduct, Performance and Ethics | BABCP members and accredited CBT practitioners | Revised December 5, 2022; an update adopted in 2026 takes effect May 1, 2027 [BABCP-Standards-2022] |
| BACP Ethical Framework for the Counselling Professions | BACP members and registrants | 2026 framework, in effect from November 3, 2026, replacing the 2018 edition [BACP-Ethical-Framework-2026] |
| HCPC Standards of conduct, performance and ethics | Registered practitioner psychologists and other professions | Effective September 1, 2024 [HCPC-SCPE-2024] |

APA's general principles (beneficence and nonmaleficence, fidelity and responsibility, integrity, justice, respect for rights and dignity) are aspirational, while its standards are enforceable [APA-Ethics-2017].

## Why it matters in CBT

CBT services often mix professions in one team. A psychologist, counselor and social worker working the same caseload may face different rules on the same dilemma, and a tool designer may need to satisfy all of them.

## Key details

| Issue | APA | ACA | NASW | UK bodies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sexual relationship with former client | Not for at least 2 years, and rarely after [APA-Ethics-2017] | Prohibited for 5 years [ACA-Ethics-2014] | Should not; worker bears the burden [NASW-Code-2021] | No sexual relationships with clients [BACP-Ethical-Framework-2026]; no abuse of position [HCPC-SCPE-2024] |
| Disclosure to prevent harm | As mandated or permitted by law [APA-Ethics-2017] | Serious and foreseeable harm [ACA-Ethics-2014] | Serious, foreseeable and imminent harm [NASW-Code-2021] | Share when people are at risk of immediate serious harm, in line with the law; know the law in both your country and the client's [BACP-Ethical-Framework-2026] |
| Technology | Electronic risks disclosed [APA-Ethics-2017] | Whole section on distance counseling and social media [ACA-Ethics-2014] | Standards apply to technology-based work [NASW-Code-2021] | Assess artificial intelligence (AI) and digital tools before use [BACP-Ethical-Framework-2026] |
| Candor when things go wrong | No named candor standard | No named candor standard | No named candor standard | Duty of candor in BABCP Standard 14 [BABCP-Standards-2022]; HCPC Standard 8 [HCPC-SCPE-2024] |

## In the session

A practical decision process, adapted from the BACP framework's questions for an ethical rationale (the issues, involving the person affected, internal and external factors, the decision, and review) [BACP-Ethical-Framework-2026]:

1. Name the dilemma and who is affected.
2. Identify the codes, laws and employer policies that apply.
3. Involve the client in the decision where it is safe and possible.
4. Consult a supervisor or ethics resource.
5. Act, then record the decision, the alternatives considered and the reasons.

- If the code sets a higher standard than the law → meet the higher ethical standard. Because the APA code says so explicitly [APA-Ethics-2017].
- If a code and the law conflict → clarify the nature of the conflict, make known your commitment to the code, take reasonable steps to resolve it consistent with the code's principles and standards, and consult. Because that is the APA conflict rule, which since 2010 no longer says you may simply follow the law and states it can never justify violating human rights [APA-Ethics-2017].
- If you hold two licenses or memberships → meet the stricter rule on each issue, consult, and record your reasoning. Because you must be able to give reasons for ethical decisions [BACP-Ethical-Framework-2026].
- If something has gone wrong with a client → tell them promptly what happened and its likely effects, and apologize. Because UK candor duties require it [BACP-Ethical-Framework-2026] [HCPC-SCPE-2024].
