**Bottom line:** Treat only populations and problems you are competent to treat by education, training, supervised experience, consultation or study, and get training, supervision or consultation before taking on new ones, or refer [APA-Ethics-2017]. Supervision is how competence is built and checked: it should be regular, matched to experience and caseload, independent of line management, and clear about who holds clinical responsibility [BACP-Ethical-Framework-2026]. Trainees must tell clients they are supervised and name the supervisor [APA-Ethics-2017].

## The principle

Competence rests on education, training, supervised experience, consultation, study or professional experience [APA-Ethics-2017]. It includes the cultural, age, disability, language and identity knowledge that a case requires [APA-Ethics-2017]. Counselors enter new specialty areas only after education, training and supervised experience [ACA-Ethics-2014]. Technology-delivered work needs its own competence, and social workers must follow the laws where they are regulated and where the client is [NASW-Code-2021]. In the United Kingdom (UK), accredited cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) practitioners must work within their scope and refer when a task is beyond it, keep knowledge and skills up to date, and effectively supervise anything they delegate [BABCP-Standards-2022].

Good supervision is itself a competence. American Psychological Association (APA) guidelines organize it into seven domains: supervisor competence, diversity, the relationship, professionalism, assessment and feedback, problems of professional competence, and ethical, legal and regulatory issues [APA-Supervision-Guidelines-2015].

## Guidance

- If a referral involves a condition or method you have not been trained in (for example an eating disorder needing medical monitoring) → refer, or accept only with a defined supervision and training plan and the client's informed agreement. Because services outside competence require relevant training, supervision or referral [APA-Ethics-2017].
- If no qualified service exists nearby → you may provide care drawing on closely related training while making a reasonable effort to gain competence through research, training, consultation or study. Because codes allow this narrow exception so care is not denied [APA-Ethics-2017].
- If an emergency arises outside your training and no other service is available → help, then hand over as soon as the emergency ends or appropriate services are available. Because emergency services outside competence must stop at that point [APA-Ethics-2017].
- If you work in an emerging area with no recognized training standards (for example care assisted by artificial intelligence (AI)) → take reasonable steps to ensure competence and protect clients. Because the code requires this in emerging areas [APA-Ethics-2017].
- If you are a trainee → tell clients at the outset that you are in training and give your supervisor's name. Because clients must know who holds legal responsibility [APA-Ethics-2017].
- If you delegate tasks (scoring measures, psychoeducation groups) → delegate only what the person can do competently with the supervision provided, and check the work. Because the delegator remains responsible [APA-Ethics-2017] [BABCP-Standards-2022].
- If personal stress, illness or grief is affecting your judgment → seek consultation and limit or suspend work as needed. Because clinicians must act when personal problems may impair their work [APA-Ethics-2017].
- If you bring session recordings or notes to supervision → explain this to clients at consent and store materials securely. Because confidentiality limits include supervision, and the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) permits psychotherapy notes in supervised training programs [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-508].
- If you plan to adopt an AI or digital tool → make sure you understand how it works and how it handles data first. Because members of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP) must assess an AI tool's risks, be competent to use it, and not defer decisions to it [BACP-Ethical-Framework-2026].
- If you are a supervisor → agree in writing what supervision covers, how feedback is given, and who holds clinical responsibility for each case. Because these are core supervision domains [APA-Supervision-Guidelines-2015] and responsibility must be agreed [BACP-Ethical-Framework-2026].

## Example phrasings

- To a client: "This is an area where I'd want specialist input. I can either work with you while my supervisor, who specializes in this, guides me, or refer you to someone who does it every day. Which would you prefer?"
- In supervision: "I'm not sure this is within my competence. Can we look at whether I should keep this case?"
- Avoid: "I've read about it, so I can do it." Reading alone rarely establishes competence for a new method.

## Evidence

These requirements are ethics code standards and professional guidelines (expert consensus). Check whether the 2015 APA supervision guidelines have since been reaffirmed or replaced. Licensure supervision hours and supervisor qualifications are set by each state board and differ widely, so check your board's current rules.
