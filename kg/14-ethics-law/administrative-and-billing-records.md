**Bottom line:** Bill the code matching the face-to-face time spent and record start and stop or total time: Medicare contractors treat 90832 as 16 to 37 minutes, 90834 as 38 to 52 and 90837 as 53 or more [CMS-A57520]. Give uninsured and self-pay clients a written Good Faith Estimate (GFE) on the federal timetable [CFR-45-149-610]. Put fees, billing and nonpayment terms in writing first [ACA-Ethics-2014]. Send payers only the minimum they need [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-502], and never let billing shape a diagnosis [ACA-Ethics-2014].

## When to use

- Setting up billing, fees or a cancellation policy.
- Choosing a code, including after a crisis session.
- Seeking authorization or booking a self-pay client.

## Steps

1. **Choose the code.** Current Procedural Terminology (CPT) codes are American Medical Association (AMA) property, paraphrased here. A time unit is reached once the midpoint is passed [AMA-CPT-Assistant-2014].

| Code | Service | Time rule |
|---|---|---|
| 90791 | Diagnostic evaluation, no medical services | Not timed; usually once at onset, again after about 6 months' gap [CMS-LCD-L33252] |
| 90832, 90834, 90837 | Psychotherapy, 30, 45, 60 minutes | 16 to 37, 38 to 52, 53 or more; never under 16 [CMS-A57520] |
| 90846, 90847 | Family psychotherapy without, with patient, 50 minutes | At least 26 minutes, the midpoint of 50 [AAPC-Family-Therapy-2017] |
| 90839 | Psychotherapy for crisis, first 60 minutes | 30 to 74 minutes [Blyler-etal-2025] |
| 90840 | Each further 30 minutes, add-on to 90839 | Beyond 74 minutes [Blyler-etal-2025] |
| 90785 | Interactive complexity, add-on | With 90791, 90832 to 90838 and group 90853; never crisis codes [CMS-A57520] [CMS-Crisis-Page] |

Do not bill crisis codes with 90791, 90792, 90785 to 90899 or 90832 to 90838. Where the non-facility rate applies outside the office, such as the client's home, Medicare uses G0017 and G0018, paid at 150% of the fee schedule amount [CMS-Crisis-Page]. Interactive complexity needs a documented factor: maladaptive communication, caregiver interference, a mandated report, or an interpreter or play equipment [CMS-LCD-L33252]. Other payers' contracts may differ.

2. **Document what auditors check.** Time, plus a treatment plan with methods, expected length and measurable goals, generally updated at least every 3 months [CMS-LCD-L33252].

3. **Obtain prior authorization** where required. Under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), preauthorization is a payment activity needing no client authorization [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-501] [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-506]. Since January 1, 2026, Medicare Advantage, Medicaid and Children's Health Insurance Program (CHIP) payers must decide within 72 hours (expedited) or 7 calendar days (standard) and give a specific denial reason [CMS-0057-F-2024].

4. **Issue a Good Faith Estimate** to clients without coverage or choosing not to file a claim: within 1 business day if booked at least 3 business days ahead, and within 3 business days if booked at least 10 business days ahead or on request [CFR-45-149-610]. Itemize service codes and charges (a diagnosis code only once made); one estimate may cover up to 12 months of recurring sessions, stating frequency and number [CMS-GFE-FAQ-2022]. Post notice of the right online and in the office, and file estimates in the record [CFR-45-149-610]. A bill at least $400 above it allows dispute resolution within 120 calendar days, pausing collection [CFR-45-149-620].

5. **Sign a fee agreement** covering fees, billing and nonpayment. Consider the client's finances, and before using collections, warn the client and offer a chance to pay [ACA-Ethics-2014].

6. **Set a cancellation policy.** Medicare beneficiaries may be charged for missed appointments only under the policy and amount applied to all patients, never billed to Medicare [CMS-MM5613]. Medicaid beneficiaries may not be charged for missed appointments, because CMS treats them as a cost of doing business, as North Carolina's Medicaid guidance states [NCDHHS-Missed-Appointments]. Check your state program.

7. **Use valid release forms:** the information, who discloses, recipient, purpose, expiry date or event, signature and date, plus revocation, conditioning and redisclosure statements; give the client a copy. Psychotherapy notes need their own authorization, and payment cannot be conditioned on it [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-508]. Federally covered substance use disorder program records need Part 2 consent [CFR-42-2-33].

**United Kingdom (UK):** National Health Service (NHS) care is free of charge except where Parliament allows [NHS-Constitution-2023], so CPT, GFEs and client billing do not apply. In private practice, cancellation charges in written terms must be fair and a genuine estimate of direct loss [CMA-Cancelling-2016].

## What to record

- Code, time, attendees and any interactive complexity factor.
- Authorization number, sessions approved and dates.
- Each GFE, signed fee agreement, release and revocation.

## Guidance

- If a session ran 52 minutes → bill 90834. Because 90837 starts at 53 minutes [CMS-A57520].
- If a client is in crisis → assess risk and safety plan first; if danger is imminent call 911 in the United States (US) or 999 in the UK, and give 988 (call or text), NHS 111 or Samaritans 116 123. Because crisis care aims to restore safety; coding comes later (see `02-risk.crisis-response`) [CMS-LCD-L33252] [Lifeline-988] [NHS-Urgent-Help] [Samaritans-Site].
- If a payer asks for full notes → send the minimum necessary summary. Because payment disclosures follow the minimum necessary rule [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-502].
- If a self-pay client asks you not to tell their health plan → agree once they pay in full. Because HIPAA requires that restriction [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-522].
- If pressed to change a diagnosis for coverage → report accurately. Because reports to insurers must be honest [ACA-Ethics-2014].
- Say: "Here is a written estimate for weekly sessions this year." Avoid: "We can sort the cost out later."

## Common problems

- Billing scheduled rather than actual time.
- No GFE for out-of-network clients paying themselves.
- Open-ended releases with no purpose or expiry.
