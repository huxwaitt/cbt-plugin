**Bottom line:** For cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) clinicians in the United States (US), the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Privacy Rule lets you share protected health information (PHI) for treatment, payment and operations without authorization, but separately kept psychotherapy notes need a specific authorization [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-508]. Clients can get copies of their record within 30 days, extendable once by 30 days [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-524]. In the United Kingdom (UK) and European Union (EU), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) treats health data as a special category, gives access within one month, and requires breach reports to the regulator within 72 hours [EU-GDPR-2016].

## What it is

HIPAA applies to "covered entities": health plans, clearinghouses, and health care providers who transmit health information electronically in standard transactions such as insurance billing [HIPAA-45-CFR-160-103]. It also binds their business associates, meaning outside parties that create, receive, maintain or transmit PHI for them [HIPAA-45-CFR-160-103]. A cash-only therapist who never bills electronically may fall outside HIPAA, but state privacy law and ethics codes still apply.

GDPR governs personal data processing in the EU. The UK applies its own version (UK GDPR) together with the Data Protection Act 2018 [UK-DPA-2018]. The Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 amends the UK rules, including time limits for and searches in response to subject access requests, and its changes are commencing in stages, so check current Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) guidance [UK-DUAA-2025].

## Why it matters in CBT

CBT generates sensitive records: thought records, formulations, risk notes, recordings and measure scores. Every disclosure, cloud tool, telehealth platform and artificial intelligence (AI) assistant is a privacy decision.

## Key details

**HIPAA (US)**

| Rule | What it requires |
|---|---|
| Treatment, payment, operations | Permitted without authorization [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-506] |
| Minimum necessary | Limit PHI to what the purpose needs; does not apply to disclosures to another provider for treatment [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-502] |
| Psychotherapy notes | Authorization needed for almost any use or disclosure (exceptions include the originator's own treatment use and supervised training programs); an authorization for them can be combined only with another psychotherapy notes authorization [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-508] |
| Serious threat | Good-faith disclosure to someone able to prevent a serious and imminent threat is permitted [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-512] |
| Right of access | Act within 30 days, one 30-day extension with written reasons; psychotherapy notes excluded; denial reviewable if access is reasonably likely to endanger life or physical safety [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-524] |
| Minors | Parents are usually personal representatives, but state law decides parental access in many cases [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-502] |
| Business associates | Written agreement required before a vendor handles PHI [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-502] |
| Breach | Notify individuals without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 days after discovery [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-404]; 500 or more people: notify the Secretary at the same time; fewer: log and report within 60 days of year end [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-408] |

Substance use disorder program records under Title 42 of the Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) Part 2 now allow a single consent for all future treatment, payment and operations, but still bar redisclosure for proceedings against the patient [CFR-42-2-33].

**GDPR and UK GDPR**

| Rule | What it requires |
|---|---|
| Lawful basis for health data | Health data is special category data; processing for health care is allowed when done by or under a professional bound by secrecy [EU-GDPR-2016]; UK condition: necessary for health or social care purposes [UK-DPA-2018] |
| Subject access | Copy of the data, first copy free, response within one month, extendable by two months for complex requests [EU-GDPR-2016] |
| Serious harm (UK) | Health data can be withheld where disclosure would cause serious harm; a non-clinician controller needs a health professional's opinion from the last 6 months [UK-DPA-2018] |
| Breach | Notify the regulator within 72 hours unless the breach is unlikely to risk people's rights [EU-GDPR-2016] |
| Fines | Up to 20 million euros or 4% of worldwide turnover [EU-GDPR-2016]; UK up to 17.5 million pounds or 4% [UK-DPA-2018] |

## In the session

- If a client asks to see their notes → explain what the record contains, offer a copy, and diarize the 30-day (US) or one-month (UK and EU) deadline. Because both regimes set response deadlines [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-524] [EU-GDPR-2016].
- If you plan to use a new app, platform or AI tool with client data → confirm a business associate agreement (US) or data processing terms (UK and EU) first. Because vendors handling PHI need written assurances [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-502].
- If a laptop with client files is lost → report to your privacy lead the same day. Because the GDPR clock is 72 hours [EU-GDPR-2016] and HIPAA notice to clients is due within 60 days at the latest [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-404].
- If an insurer asks for "all notes" → send what the request needs, not psychotherapy notes. Because minimum necessary applies [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-502] and psychotherapy notes need specific authorization [HIPAA-45-CFR-164-508].
