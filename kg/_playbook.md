# Playbook: the episode of care in order

This file gives the order of work in one cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) episode of care, from first
contact to follow-up. Each step says what to do, what to capture, and which pages to read. It is a map, not
a protocol: condition pages set the specific dose and techniques, and clinical judgment, supervision and
local policy decide.

**Risk overrides order.** At any step, if a client discloses suicidal thoughts, self-harm, intent to harm
someone, or abuse, pause the step and follow `02-risk.crisis-response` or `02-risk.suicide-risk-assessment`.
Crisis contacts: US 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988) or 911; UK 999, NHS 111 (mental health
option) or Samaritans 116 123.

## 1. First contact

- **Do:** confirm the client's location, a safe contact number, who referred them and why, and whether the
  service fits (scope, urgency, setting). For remote work, check privacy and location at the start.
- **Capture:** contact and emergency details, referral question, urgency, preferred language and any need
  for an interpreter or adaptation.
- **Read:** `01-intake.first-session-structure`, `05-session-structure.telehealth-adaptations`,
  `13-context-culture-development.working-with-interpreters`, `14-ethics-law.telehealth-law`.

## 2. Intake and consent

- **Do:** obtain informed consent to assessment, explain confidentiality and its limits, set expectations
  for the evaluation, and gather the presenting problem and history.
- **Capture:** signed or recorded consent, limits of confidentiality explained, presenting problem with one
  specific recent example, history (psychiatric, medical, substance, family, social, trauma, strengths),
  fees and the cancellation policy, and in the US a Good Faith Estimate for uninsured or self-pay clients.
- **Read:** `14-ethics-law.informed-consent`, `14-ethics-law.confidentiality-and-limits`,
  `01-intake.presenting-problem`, `01-intake.history-taking`, `01-intake.intake-record-checklist`,
  `12-medical-pharmacology.medication-history`, `14-ethics-law.administrative-and-billing-records`.

## 3. Risk screen

- **Do:** ask every new client directly about suicidal thoughts, self-harm, harm to others and
  safeguarding. A positive screen leads to a full assessment the same session.
- **Capture:** the screen answers in the client's words, any warning signs, protective factors, the
  decision taken and why. Do not record a low, medium or high label as a prediction.
- **Read:** `01-intake.risk-screen-at-intake`, `02-risk.asking-about-suicide`, `02-risk.c-ssrs`,
  `02-risk.suicide-risk-assessment`, `02-risk.safety-planning`, `02-risk.harm-to-others`,
  `02-risk.safeguarding`, `02-risk.risk-documentation`.

## 4. Assessment and baseline measures

- **Do:** gather the information needed to diagnose and formulate: a structured interview where useful,
  collateral information with consent, medical review where physical causes are possible, and baseline
  standardized and personal measures.
- **Capture:** interview results, informant reports, medical red flags and referrals, baseline scores
  (for example PHQ-9 and GAD-7) and idiographic ratings, maintaining processes noticed so far.
- **Read:** `01-intake.structured-interviews`, `01-intake.collateral-information`,
  `08-measurement.instrument-selection`, `08-measurement.idiographic-measures`,
  `12-medical-pharmacology.medical-mimics`, `12-medical-pharmacology.when-to-refer-medically`,
  `15-transdiagnostic-processes.assessing-transdiagnostic-processes`, and the condition leaf's
  `assessment` page (for example `depression.assessment`). In pregnancy or the year after birth add
  `13-context-culture-development.perinatal-mental-health`; for an anxious child add
  `13-context-culture-development.child-anxiety`.

## 5. Diagnosis

- **Do:** reason through the differential (substances and medicines, medical conditions, the best-fitting
  disorder, normal reactions), decide what to treat first when several disorders are present, consider
  culture, and share the diagnosis in plain words. Diagnose only within your scope of practice.
- **Capture:** working and provisional diagnoses with the evidence for each, rule-outs, codes if needed,
  cultural formulation notes, how the client understood the diagnosis.
- **Read:** `10-diagnosis.differential-diagnosis-process`, `10-diagnosis.comorbidity`,
  `10-diagnosis.cultural-formulation-interview`, `10-diagnosis.communicating-a-diagnosis`,
  `10-diagnosis.icd-10-cm-and-icd-11`, `10-diagnosis.scope-of-practice-diagnosis`, and the condition leaf's
  `overview` page.

## 6. Formulation

- **Do:** build a shared explanation with the client, starting descriptive (one episode in a five-part map)
  and adding maintaining cycles, then beliefs and history only as needed. Choose a disorder-specific model
  when one diagnosis explains most problems. Include strengths.
- **Capture:** the formulation diagram in the client's words, the maintaining processes it names, the
  client's agreement and doubts. `formulation\index.html` records one against a chosen model.
- **Read:** `03-formulation.what-is-case-formulation`, `03-formulation.five-part-model`,
  `03-formulation.maintenance-cycles`, `03-formulation.choosing-a-model`,
  `03-formulation.sharing-the-formulation`, `03-formulation.strengths-in-formulation`, and the condition
  leaf's `cbt-model` page.

## 7. Treatment planning

- **Do:** turn the problem list into specific, measurable goals; choose a guideline-recommended treatment at
  the right intensity; plan session number, frequency and review points; decide how therapy fits with any
  medication; say when CBT is not the right treatment now.
- **Capture:** problem list, goals, chosen treatment and its evidence status, planned dose and review dates,
  measures to track, the client's consent to the plan, all written into the treatment plan document.
- **Read:** `04-treatment-planning.problem-list-and-goals`, `04-treatment-planning.stepped-care`,
  `04-treatment-planning.guideline-map`, `04-treatment-planning.guideline-map-other-conditions`,
  `04-treatment-planning.session-planning`, `04-treatment-planning.treatment-plan-document`,
  `04-treatment-planning.combining-with-medication`,
  `04-treatment-planning.when-cbt-is-not-indicated`, `03-formulation.formulation-to-treatment`.

## 8. Socializing to CBT

- **Do:** explain the cognitive model with the client's own example, how sessions run, the role of
  homework, feedback and collaboration, and what to expect. Build hope with a credible rationale.
- **Capture:** the client's understanding in their words, concerns about the approach, agreed ways of
  working (session length, contact between sessions, recordings).
- **Read:** `01-intake.socializing-to-cbt`, `01-intake.goals-and-expectations`,
  `alliance-and-stance.hope-and-expectancy`, `alliance-and-stance.collaborative-empiricism`,
  `responding-in-session.psychoeducation-without-lecturing`.

## 9. Active treatment sessions

- **Do:** run each session to the standard structure: mood check and measures, bridge, agenda, homework
  review, work on one or two agenda items with the planned techniques, summaries, new homework, feedback.
  Respond to the client turn by turn with reflections, validation and Socratic questions.
- **Capture:** a session note with scores, what was worked on, the homework agreed, risk changes and the
  plan for next time.
- **Read:** `05-session-structure.session-structure`, `05-session-structure.mood-check-and-bridge`,
  `05-session-structure.agenda-setting`, `05-session-structure.homework-design`,
  `05-session-structure.homework-review-and-adherence`, `05-session-structure.session-summaries-and-feedback`,
  `05-session-structure.session-notes`, `responding-in-session.core-response-skills`,
  `responding-in-session.socratic-dialogue`, `responding-in-session.validation`,
  `alliance-and-stance.therapeutic-alliance`, and the technique pages the plan names (for example
  `cognitive.thought-records`, `behavioral.behavioral-activation`, `behavioral.exposure-principles`) plus
  the condition leaf's `treatment` page.

## 10. Monitoring and non-response

- **Do:** review scores every session against the expected course, ask for alliance feedback, and repeat the
  risk check when scores or hopelessness rise. At a planned review, or when a client is not on track,
  revisit the formulation, the alliance, adherence and dose, then adjust, step up or refer.
- **Capture:** score trend, reliable change status, rupture and repair events, the review decision and why.
- **Read:** `08-measurement.measurement-based-care`, `08-measurement.reliable-and-clinically-significant-change`,
  `08-measurement.process-measures`, `alliance-and-stance.outcome-and-alliance-feedback`,
  `alliance-and-stance.rupture-and-repair`, `04-treatment-planning.managing-non-response`,
  `02-risk.ongoing-risk-monitoring`, `05-session-structure.therapist-competence`.

## 11. Ending and relapse prevention

- **Do:** decide with the client when to end, using goals and measures; taper sessions; write a relapse
  prevention blueprint in the client's words; explain lapse versus relapse; arrange handover if care
  continues elsewhere; write the discharge summary.
- **Capture:** end scores and the outcome classification, the blueprint, early warning signs and setback
  plan, crisis contacts, referrals made, the discharge summary.
- **Read:** `09-ending.ending-criteria`, `09-ending.planning-the-ending`,
  `09-ending.relapse-prevention-blueprint`, `09-ending.lapse-relapse-and-recurrence`,
  `09-ending.discharge-summary`, `09-ending.referral-and-handover`, `09-ending.dropout-and-unplanned-endings`,
  `14-ethics-law.record-keeping`.

## 12. Follow-up

- **Do:** offer booster or continuation sessions where relapse risk is high, repeat measures, rehearse the
  blueprint, and restart treatment if a relapse is confirmed. Keep records for the required period.
- **Capture:** booster scores, blueprint updates, any re-referral decision.
- **Read:** `09-ending.booster-sessions`, `09-ending.lapse-relapse-and-recurrence`,
  `depression.relapse-prevention-mbct`, `14-ethics-law.record-keeping`.

## Across every step

- Culture, identity, age and neurodivergence shape every step: `13-context-culture-development.cultural-humility`,
  `13-context-culture-development.culturally-adapted-cbt`, and the adaptation pages for children, older
  adults, autism, intellectual disability and the perinatal period.
- Physical illness and bereavement shape every step: `chronic-pain-and-physical-health.overview` and
  `grief-and-loss.overview`.
- Ethics and law apply throughout: `14-ethics-law.boundaries-and-dual-relationships`,
  `14-ethics-law.competence-and-supervision`, `14-ethics-law.digital-and-ai-tools`.
- Medication changes at any point: `12-medical-pharmacology.medication-and-cbt-interactions`,
  `12-medical-pharmacology.working-with-prescribers`, `12-medical-pharmacology.discontinuation-and-withdrawal`.
