# 06-therapeutic-relationship/alliance-and-stance: Alliance and Therapist Stance

## This surface
The evidence-based relationship factors and the therapist stance that predict outcome in cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT): the alliance, empathy, positive regard, genuineness, collaborative empiricism, hope and expectancy, client feedback, and the interpersonal skills of effective therapists. It also covers how to recognize and repair ruptures, how to handle self-disclosure and boundaries, and which therapist behaviors harm the relationship. It applies at every stage of the episode of care; for moment-to-moment response skills use the sibling leaf `06-therapeutic-relationship/responding-in-session`.

## Pages in order
1. `alliance-and-stance.therapeutic-alliance`: start here for what the alliance is and why it predicts outcome and dropout.
2. `alliance-and-stance.collaborative-empiricism`: the CBT-specific form of the alliance, working as a team to test beliefs.
3. `alliance-and-stance.empathy`: how to show and check understanding before and during techniques.
4. `alliance-and-stance.positive-regard-and-genuineness`: warmth that does not depend on compliance, and honest congruence.
5. `alliance-and-stance.hope-and-expectancy`: a credible rationale and realistic hope, including when hopelessness signals risk.
6. `alliance-and-stance.outcome-and-alliance-feedback`: session-by-session outcome and alliance measurement and what to do with it.
7. `alliance-and-stance.rupture-and-repair`: the procedure when the client withdraws or confronts.
8. `alliance-and-stance.what-harms-the-relationship`: behaviors to avoid and what to do instead.
9. `alliance-and-stance.self-disclosure-and-boundaries`: when to share, how to answer personal questions, crossings versus violations.
10. `alliance-and-stance.facilitative-interpersonal-skills`: what distinguishes more effective therapists and how to develop the skills.

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Pushing Technique Through a Rupture | The client pushes back or goes quiet during a technique, the therapist argues the rationale harder, and the strain grows until homework stops or the client drops out. | `alliance-and-stance.pitfall-pushing-technique-through-a-rupture` |
| Assuming the Alliance Is Fine | The therapist judges the relationship good without asking, the client seems polite and compliant, and stalled progress, deterioration or dropout arrive as a surprise. | `alliance-and-stance.pitfall-assuming-the-alliance-is-fine` |
| Collecting Feedback Without Using It | Outcome and alliance forms are completed every session but scores are never discussed, perfect or flat ratings go unexamined, and the plan does not change when progress stalls. | `alliance-and-stance.pitfall-collecting-feedback-without-using-it` |
