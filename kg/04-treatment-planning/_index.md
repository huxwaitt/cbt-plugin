# 04-treatment-planning: Treatment Planning

## This stage
Treatment planning turns the assessment and formulation into an agreed plan: what to work on, which guideline-recommended treatment at what intensity and dose, how it fits with medication, and how progress will be reviewed. It sits between formulation and the first intervention session, and it is revisited at every formal review and when a client is not improving.

## Decisions in order
1. Which problems will therapy target, in what order, and what goals and measures will show progress? → `04-treatment-planning.problem-list-and-goals`
2. Is CBT the right treatment now, or should something come first, be adapted, or be referred elsewhere? → `04-treatment-planning.when-cbt-is-not-indicated`
3. What do guidelines recommend for this condition and severity? → `04-treatment-planning.guideline-map` and `04-treatment-planning.guideline-map-other-conditions`
4. What level of intensity should treatment start at, and when should it step up? → `04-treatment-planning.stepped-care`
5. Should CBT be combined with medication, and in what sequence? → `04-treatment-planning.combining-with-medication`
6. How many sessions, how often, in what phases, and when are the reviews? → `04-treatment-planning.session-planning`
7. How do I write the agreed plan up as a record for the chart, a payer, or a UK care plan? → `04-treatment-planning.treatment-plan-document`
8. The client is not improving: what to check and what to change → `04-treatment-planning.managing-non-response`

## Pitfalls
| Pitfall | Symptom | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Vague or Therapist-Imposed Goals | Goals read like "feel better" or "reduce anxiety," were chosen by the therapist, have no measure or date, and the client is polite but not practicing. | `04-treatment-planning.pitfall-vague-or-imposed-goals` |
| Mismatched Treatment Intensity | A client with severe or complex problems is cycled through brief self-help, or a client with mild symptoms gets long open-ended therapy, and nobody revisits the fit. | `04-treatment-planning.pitfall-mismatched-intensity` |
| Continuing Treatment Without Review | Sessions roll on past the planned number with flat or worsening scores, no review date, and a therapist who believes the client is improving. | `04-treatment-planning.pitfall-continuing-without-review` |
