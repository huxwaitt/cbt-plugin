**Bottom line:** Formulation is treated as a core cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) skill, but direct evidence that it improves outcomes is weak. Therapists agree on descriptive content and disagree on inferred beliefs and mechanisms; training and experience improve quality; formulation-guided CBT does about as well as protocol CBT; and the best-supported element is routine progress monitoring. Practice should favor simple, collaborative, testable formulations.

## What it is

This page summarizes four research questions: Is formulation reliable? Is it valid and of good quality? Does it improve outcomes? How do clients experience it? A 2018 systematic review found 24 eligible studies, mostly about formulation as assessment; few examined its use in session or its link to outcomes. Experienced therapists can reliably construct some elements, but its importance for CBT efficacy is not yet demonstrated [Easden-Kazantzis-2018].

## Why it matters in CBT

Claims that formulation is the "heart" of CBT run ahead of the data. Kuyken and colleagues describe strong evidence that conceptualization enhances outcomes as "strikingly absent" [Kuyken-etal-2008]. Padesky, in contrast, writes that there appears to be a link between high-quality conceptualization and outcomes [Padesky-2020]. Both positions can be held: the evidence base is thin, and what exists supports formulations that are collaborative, simple and tested against data.

## Key details

**Reliability and quality.**
- Therapists agree on presenting problems; agreement falls for inferred elements such as core beliefs and maintaining factors [Kuyken-etal-2008].
- Of 115 practitioners formulating the same case on the Beck diagram, only 44% produced a formulation rated at least good enough. Reliability and quality were higher with experience and accreditation [Kuyken-etal-2005].

**Training.** After a 2-hour training session, 20 clinicians wrote formulations rated higher in quality than 23 untrained clinicians; the average trained clinician outperformed 86% of controls [Kendjelic-Eells-2007].

**Competence measurement.** The Collaborative Case Conceptualization Rating Scale (CCC-RS) has 14 items, each rated 0 (incompetent) to 3 (expert), summed in four areas: levels of conceptualization, collaboration, empiricism, and strengths and resilience (maximum 42) [Padesky-etal-2011]. In 40 sessions from a trial of CBT for treatment-resistant depression it showed internal consistency of .94 and inter-rater reliability of .84, and correlated .54 with general CBT competence [Kuyken-etal-2016a]. It is not yet linked to outcomes.

**Outcomes.**
| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| 58 anxious and depressed outpatients, formulation-driven CBT with weekly outcome monitoring in private practice (40 also received adjunct treatments such as medication) | Improvement broadly comparable to randomized trials; uncontrolled [Persons-etal-2006] |
| 50 patients with bulimia nervosa randomized to manual or individualized CBT | Few differences; a few outcomes and response favored individualized; 80% of non-responders were in the manual arm [Ghaderi-2006] |
| Formulation in CBT for psychosis | Little or no effect on symptoms; therapists rated the relationship as improved [Chadwick-etal-2003] |
| Review of randomized comparisons | Formulation-guided treatment generally similar to, occasionally better than, standardized treatment; none found standardized superior; strongest support for the feedback element [Persons-Hong-2016; Persons-Tompkins-2022] |

**Client experience.** Most clients describe better understanding and feeling understood, but formulation can cause distress, sometimes lasting [Redhead-etal-2015].

## In the session

- If you are tempted to present a detailed belief-level formulation early → keep to what the client has described. Because inferred elements are where reliability is poorest [Kuyken-etal-2005].
- If you supervise or train → rate formulation competence on recorded sessions, not written formulations alone. Because a reliable observer-rated scale exists [Kuyken-etal-2016a].
- If you design a formulation tool → build in client confirmation, uncertainty marks and progress data, rather than auto-generated belief statements. Because the evidence favors collaboration and monitoring over confident inference [Persons-Hong-2016].
- If a colleague claims individualized therapy is clearly better than the protocol → point out that trials mostly show similar outcomes, with occasional small advantages for individualized treatment. Because no study has shown that individualized treatment unambiguously improves outcomes [Kuyken-etal-2008], and no trial has found standardized treatment better [Persons-Hong-2016].
